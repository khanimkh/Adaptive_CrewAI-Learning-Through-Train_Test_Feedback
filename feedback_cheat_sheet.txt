Adaptive-CrewAI Training Feedback Cheat Sheet

Use this template whenever CrewAI asks for human feedback during train().

CORE TEMPLATE
Score: <0-10>
What was good: <2-3 concrete strengths>
What to improve: <2-3 concrete gaps>
Required fix for next iteration: <specific, measurable change>

QUICK RUBRIC (0-10 each)
1) Accuracy: Are findings factually correct and aligned with ticket data?
2) Actionability: Are actions executable, prioritized, and realistic?
3) Completeness: Are owners, timelines, KPIs, and risks included?
4) Clarity: Is output concise, structured, and easy for stakeholders?

EXAMPLE FEEDBACKS TO PASTE

A) For triage output
Score: 7/10
What was good: Correctly identified priority clusters and highlighted critical issues.
What to improve: Missing root-cause hypotheses per issue type and impact quantification.
Required fix for next iteration: Add issue-wise root cause, affected users, and expected business impact.

B) For action strategy output
Score: 8/10
What was good: Recommendations are practical and mostly prioritized.
What to improve: Missing explicit owner per action and SLA targets.
Required fix for next iteration: Add owner, SLA, escalation trigger, and success KPI for each action.

C) For final report output
Score: 9/10
What was good: Strong structure with clear findings and a useful plan.
What to improve: Risk mitigations and fallback plans are not explicit.
Required fix for next iteration: Add risk table with mitigation, fallback, and monitoring metric for each high-risk issue.

HIGH-QUALITY PROMPTS YOU CAN REUSE
- "Add a 30-day implementation table with weekly milestones, owners, KPIs, and dependencies."
- "Quantify expected impact: target resolution time, CSAT improvement, and ticket reduction by category."
- "Include 3 key risks, mitigation steps, fallback plan, and alert thresholds."
- "Add an executive summary in 5 bullets for leadership review."

DO / DON'T
DO:
- Be specific and measurable.
- Keep one required fix that is testable in next iteration.
- Keep scoring consistent across iterations.

DON'T:
- Give vague feedback (e.g., "make it better").
- Change criteria every iteration.
- Focus only on style while skipping correctness and KPI gaps.

MINI ONE-LINER (if you want fast feedback)
Score: 8/10 | Good structure and prioritization | Missing owners + KPI targets + fallback plan | Next iteration must include owner/SLA/KPI/risk table per issue.
